Anger displaced her thirst: for now the daughter of Coeus
no longer begged the unworthy men, nor could she bear to speak any longer
words beneath a goddess; raising her palms to the stars,
she said, “May you live forever in that pool.”
The goddess’s wish came true: they delight to be beneath the water
and now to submerge all their limbs in the hollow marsh,
now to raise their heads, now to swim on the surface,
often to stand on the bank of the pool, often
to leap back into the cool pond. But even now they exercise
their ugly tongues in quarrels and, all shame cast aside,
though they are beneath the water, try to hurl abuse beneath the water.
Their voice too is now hoarse, and their swollen necks puff up,
and their very insults widen their gaping mouths.
Their backs touch their heads; their necks seem to have disappeared;
their spine turns green, while the belly, the largest part of the body, turns white,
and as new-made frogs they leap into the muddy water.”
