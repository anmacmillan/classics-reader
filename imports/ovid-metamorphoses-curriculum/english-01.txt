Then indeed they all feared the manifest anger of the divine power
women and men alike, and everyone honoured with greater devotion
the mighty divinity of the goddess who bore the twins,
and, as happens, from the more recent deed they retold earlier ones.
One of them said: “In fertile Lycia too,
long ago, farmers did not scorn the goddess without punishment.
The story is little known, indeed, because the men were obscure,
but it is wondrous. I myself saw the pool and the place
famous for the marvel. For my father, now advanced in age
and unable to bear the journey, had ordered me to bring back
some choice cattle from there, and as I went he himself had given me
a guide from that people. While with him I surveyed the pastures,
behold, in the middle of a lake stood an ancient altar
black with sacrificial ash and surrounded by trembling reeds.
My guide halted and murmured fearfully, “Be gracious to me,”
and I likewise murmured, “Be gracious.”
