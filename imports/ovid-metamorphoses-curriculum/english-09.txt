Now he uses endearments, now brings it gifts pleasing to girls:
shells and polished pebbles,
small birds, and flowers of a thousand colours,
lilies, painted balls, and tears fallen from the tree
of the Heliades; he also adorns its limbs with clothing,
puts gems on its fingers and long necklaces around its neck;
light pearls hang from its ears and ribbons upon its breast.
Everything suits her; nor does she seem less beautiful naked.
He places her on covers dyed with Sidonian purple,
calls her the companion of his bed, and sets her reclining neck
upon soft feathers, as if she could feel.
