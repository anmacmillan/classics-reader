First, the sky and the lands and the watery plains,
the shining globe of the Moon and the Titan's stars
a spirit within nourishes, and mind, poured through all the limbs,
sets the whole mass in motion and mingles with the great body.
From this come the race of men and of beasts, and the lives of flying creatures,
and the monsters the sea carries beneath its marble-smooth surface.
Fiery is the energy in these seeds, and heavenly the origin
of them, so far as harmful bodies do not slow them down
and earthly limbs and dying members do not dull them.
Hence they fear and desire, grieve and rejoice, and the open air
they cannot make out, shut up in darkness and a blind prison.
What is more, when on their last day life has left them,
even then not all evil leaves the poor souls, nor do all
the plagues of the body depart completely, and it must be that deep within
much that has long grown in becomes ingrained in wondrous ways.
So they are disciplined with punishments, and for old wrongs
they pay the penalty: some are hung out, spread empty
and suspended to the winds; for others, beneath a vast whirlpool,
the ingrained guilt is washed away, or burned out by fire;
each of us suffers his own spirit's fate; then through wide
Elysium we are sent, and a few of us hold the happy fields,
until the long lapse of days, when the cycle of time is complete,
has removed the ingrained stain, and leaves pure
the heavenly sense and the fire of unmixed air.
All these, when they have turned the wheel for a thousand years,
a god calls out to the river Lethe in a great throng,
so that, remembering nothing, they may see the vault above again
and begin to wish to return into bodies once more.
