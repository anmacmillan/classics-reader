Her sister heard, and half-dead with fear, in a frantic rush,
tearing her face with her nails and beating her breast with her fists,
she runs through the crowd and calls the dying woman by name:
"So this was what it meant, sister? Were you deceiving me?
Was this what that pyre, what the fires and altars were preparing for me?
What shall I, abandoned, lament first? Did you scorn your sister
as your companion in death? You should have called me to the same fate:
the same pain, the same hour would have taken us both by the sword.
Did I even build it with these hands, and call aloud on our fathers'
gods, so that I, cruel one, would be absent while you lay there?
You have destroyed yourself and me, sister, and the people and the elders
of Sidon, and your city. Give me water, let me wash
the wounds, and if any last breath still hovers over her,
catch it with my lips." So saying, she had climbed the high steps,
and she held her half-dead sister in her arms and cradled her
with a groan, and dried the dark blood with her dress.
Dido, trying to raise her heavy eyes, again
sinks back; the wound fixed deep in her breast hisses.
Three times, lifting herself, she rose up, propped on her elbow;
three times she rolled back onto the couch, and with wandering eyes in the high
heaven she sought the light, and groaned when she had found it.
Then almighty Juno, pitying her long suffering
and her hard death, sent Iris down from Olympus
to set free the struggling soul from the limbs that held it.
For since she was dying neither by fate nor by a deserved death,
but wretchedly before her time, set ablaze by sudden madness,
Proserpina had not yet taken the golden lock from her head
nor condemned her life to Stygian Orcus.
So Iris, dewy, on saffron wings through the sky,
trailing a thousand varied colours against the sun opposite,
flies down and stood above her head: "This lock, sacred to Dis,
I take as I was ordered, and I release you from that body."
So she speaks, and with her right hand cuts the lock: and at once
all warmth slipped away, and her life withdrew into the winds.
