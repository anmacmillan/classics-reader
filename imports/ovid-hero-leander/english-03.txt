And now, with my arms tired beneath each shoulder,
I raised myself strongly, high upon the surface of the water.
When I saw the light far away, I said, “My fire is in it;
those shores hold my light!”
Suddenly strength returned to my weary arms,
and the water seemed gentler to me than it had been.
Love, which burns in my eager heart, ensures
that I cannot feel the cold of the chilly deep.
The nearer I come and the closer the shore becomes,
the less remains, the more I delight in going on.
But as soon as I too can be seen, you immediately add
courage as my spectator and make me strong.
Even as I swim I now strive to please my mistress,
and toss my arms before your eyes.
Your nurse can scarcely stop you from descending into the deep—
for I saw this too, and I was not deceiving myself.
Yet, although she held you back as you went,
she did not prevent your foot from growing wet at the water’s edge.
You receive me in an embrace and join happy kisses—
kisses, great gods, worth seeking across the sea!—
