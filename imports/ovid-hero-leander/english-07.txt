The greeting you sent me in words, Leander—
come, so that I may possess the greeting you sent in reality!
Every delay that postpones our joy is long to me.
Forgive my confession: I do not love patiently!
We burn with equal fire, but I am unequal to you in strength;
I suspect that men have a stronger spirit.
As the body of tender girls is weak, so too is their mind—
add a little delay and I shall fail!
You men spend long delays on different pursuits,
now hunting, now cultivating the fertile countryside.
Either the law courts detain you or the rewards of the oiled wrestling-ground,
or you bend the neck of an obedient horse with the rein;
now you catch a bird with a snare, now a fish with a hook;
a later hour is washed away with wine set before you.
Deprived of these things, even if I were burning less fiercely,
nothing remains for me to do except love.
I do what remains, and I love you, my only delight,
even more than could be returned to me!
