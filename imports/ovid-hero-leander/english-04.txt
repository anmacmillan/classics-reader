The night, we ourselves, and the knowing tower know the rest,
as does the light that shows me my path through the shallows.
The joys of that night can no more be numbered
than the seaweed of the Hellespontine sea;
the less time we were given for our secret love,
the more carefully we ensured it was not wasted.
And now, as Tithonus’s wife was about to drive away the night,
Lucifer, forerunner of Dawn, had risen;
we heaped up hurried kisses, snatched without order,
and complained that nights grant so little delay.
At last, after lingering despite the nurse’s bitter warning,
I left the tower and made for the cold shore.
We parted in tears, and I returned to the virgin’s sea,
looking back at my mistress for as long as I could.
If any trust is placed in truth, as I come from here I seem to be a swimmer;
when I return, I seem to myself a shipwrecked man.
