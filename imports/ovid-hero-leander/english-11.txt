Grant pardon and save two lives! He swims, but on those same waters
Leander’s body and my hope both depend.
Look, the lamp has crackled—for we are writing with it beside us—
it has crackled and given us favourable signs.
See, the nurse pours wine into the auspicious flames;
“Tomorrow,” she says, “we shall be more,” and she herself drinks.
Make us more numerous by gliding across the conquered sea,
you who have been received deep within my whole heart!
Return to your camp, deserter from our shared love;
why are my limbs laid in the middle of the bed alone?
There is nothing to fear! Venus herself will favour the bold,
and, born from the sea, will smooth the sea’s pathways.
Often I myself long to go through the middle of the waves,
but this strait is usually safer for men.
For why, when Phrixus and the sister of Phrixus travelled here,
did the woman alone give her name to the vast waters?
Perhaps you fear that there will not be time for the return,
or that you cannot endure the burden of a double labour.
Then let us set out from opposite shores and meet in mid-sea,
and give each other kisses on the surface of the water;
then let each of us return again to our own city:
that will be little, but more than nothing!
