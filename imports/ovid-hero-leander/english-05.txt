I almost touch with my hand what I love, so close is it;
but often, alas, this “almost” brings tears to my eyes!
What else is it to want to seize fruit that flees
and to pursue the hope of a river that retreats from one’s mouth?
So shall I never hold you unless the wave permits it,
and will no winter ever see me happy?
Since nothing is less constant than wind and wave,
must my hope always rest upon winds and water?
Yet it is still summer. What will happen when the Pleiad, the Bear-keeper,
and the Olenian Goat have battered the sea for me?
Either I do not know how reckless I am, or love
will send me heedlessly into the straits even then.
Do not think I promise this because that season is far away;
I shall not be slow to give you proof of my promise.
Let the sea swell for a few nights more;
I shall attempt to go through the resisting waters.
Either my boldness will succeed and leave me safe,
or death will be the end of my anxious love!
Yet I shall pray to be cast upon your side,
and for your harbour to hold my shipwrecked limbs;
for you will weep, honour my body with your touch,
and say, “I was the cause of this man’s death!”
