Yet, young conqueror of the swelling waters,
despise the straits in such a way that you still fear them!
Ships fashioned with skill are sunk by the sea;
do you think your arms can do more than oars?
What you desire, Leander—to swim—is what sailors fear;
this is usually the end for shattered ships.
Poor me! I wish not to persuade you of what I urge,
and I pray that you yourself may be braver than my warnings—
provided only that you arrive, and that your arms, often tossed by the waves,
may fall weary around my shoulders!
