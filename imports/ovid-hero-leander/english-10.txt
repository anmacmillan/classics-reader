So, when the greater part of the night has passed in disappointment,
sleep secretly steals upon my weary eyes.
Perhaps, rascal, you sleep with me unwillingly nevertheless,
and although you do not wish to come yourself, you come.
For now I seem to see you already swimming close by,
now to feel your wet arms upon my shoulders,
now to give the coverings I usually give to your dripping limbs,
now to warm our joined breasts in my embrace,
and many things besides that a modest tongue must keep silent,
which it pleases me to have done but shames me to recount.
Poor me! This pleasure is brief and unreal;
for you always depart together with my sleep.
Oh, let us eager lovers at last be joined more firmly,
and let our joy not lack true certainty!
