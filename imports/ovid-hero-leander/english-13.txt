But whenever I turn towards the blue waves,
some unknown chill numbs my fearful heart.
Nor am I less disturbed by the vision of last night,
although I have atoned for it with my sacred rites.
For near dawn, when the lamp was already growing sleepy,
at the time when dreams are usually seen as true,
the threads fell from my fingers, loosened by sleep,
and I let my neck be supported by the pillow.
Then I seemed to see a dolphin swimming through the wind-driven waves,
with a confidence that appeared to me free from doubt;
after a wave dashed it against the thirsty sand,
the water and its life abandoned the wretched creature together.
Whatever it means, I am afraid; do not laugh at my dreams,
and entrust your arms only to a calm sea!
If you do not spare yourself, spare the girl you love,
who will never be safe unless you are safe!
Yet there is hope that calm is near for the broken waves;
cut through the peaceful path with your whole breast!
Meanwhile, since the straits are not passable for the swimmer,
let this letter soothe the hateful delay.
