Either I whisper about you with my white-haired nurse
and wonder what cause is delaying your journey;
or, looking out over the sea, stirred by the hateful wind,
I rebuke the waters in almost your own words;
or, when the heavy wave has relaxed a little of its fury,
I complain that you could come but do not wish to;
and as I complain, tears flow through my loving eyes,
which my old confidante dries with a trembling thumb.
Often I look to see whether your footprints are on the shore,
as though the sand could preserve the marks impressed upon it;
and, so that I may ask about you and write to you, I ask whether anyone
has come from Abydos, or whether anyone is going to Abydos.
