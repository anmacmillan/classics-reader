Tell me, Muse, of the man of many turns, who very far and wide
was driven to wander, after he had sacked the sacred citadel of Troy;
and he saw the cities of many men and came to know their mind,
and many the woes he suffered on the sea in his heart,
striving to win his own life and the homecoming of his companions.
But not even so did he save his companions, though he longed to;
for by their own recklessness they perished,
fools, who the cattle of Hyperion the Sun
devoured; and he took from them the day of their homecoming.
Of these things, from some point at least, goddess, daughter of Zeus, tell us also.
