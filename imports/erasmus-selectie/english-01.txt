The night was faintly lit, and one of the sailors was standing at the top of the mast in the crow's nest (the galea, as they call it, I believe), looking round to see if he could spot any land: a ball of fire began to hover beside him. For sailors that is the grimmest of omens, whenever the fire is single; a happy one, when there are two.
