So the Greeks at Thermopylae fought in this way. Xerxes summoned Demaratus and began questioning him, starting as follows.
“Demaratus, you are a good man.
I judge this by the truth:
for everything you said has turned out just so.
But now tell me: how many of the Lacedaemonians are left, and how many of them are such fighters in war as these, or are they all?”
He replied: “My king, the whole body of the Lacedaemonians is large, and their cities are many;
but what you wish to learn, you shall know.
In Lacedaemon there is a city, Sparta, of about eight thousand men, and all of these are equal to those who fought here;
the other Lacedaemonians are not equal to these, but they are brave.”
To this Xerxes said: “Demaratus, in what way shall we overcome these men with the least trouble?
Come, explain it to me;
for you know the ins and outs of their plans, since you were their king.”
He answered: “My king, if you truly seek my advice in earnest, it is right that I tell you what is best:
that you should send three hundred ships of your fleet against the Laconian land.
Off its coast lies an island called Cythera, of which Chilon, the wisest man among us, used to say that it would be a greater gain for the Spartans if it had sunk beneath the sea than if it stood above it, for he always expected something to come from it such as I am now describing to you; not that he foresaw your expedition, but he feared every expedition of men alike.
Let your men set out from this island and keep the Lacedaemonians in fear.
With a war on their own doorstep, at home, you need not fear that they will come to the help of the rest of Greece while it is being conquered by your army.
And once the rest of Greece is enslaved, Laconia is left alone and by then weak.
But if you do not do this, expect the following to happen.
There is a narrow isthmus leading to the Peloponnese;
in that place, when all the Peloponnesians have sworn an alliance against you, expect other battles, fiercer than those that have already been fought.
But if you do the other thing, both this isthmus and the cities will come over to you without a fight.”
