It was early in the morning, Abraham rose early, he embraced Sarah, the bride of his old age, and Sarah kissed Isaac, who had taken away her disgrace, her pride, her hope in all generations.
So they rode in silence along the way, and Abraham's gaze was fixed on the ground, until on the 4th day, then he lifted up his eyes and saw far off Mount Moriah, but his gaze turned again toward the ground.
Silently he laid the firewood in order, bound Isaac, silently he drew the knife; then he saw the ram that God had provided.
That he offered up and went home.
– – – From that day on Abraham became old; he could not forget that God had demanded it of him.
Isaac thrived as before; but Abraham's eye was darkened, he saw joy no more.
When the child has grown big and is to be weaned, the mother virginally hides her breast, so the child has no mother any more.
Happy the child who did not lose its mother in some other way!
