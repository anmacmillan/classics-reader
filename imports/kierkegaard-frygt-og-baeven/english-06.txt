It was early in the morning, everything was prepared for the journey in Abraham's house.
He took leave of Sarah, and Eliezer, the faithful servant, followed him out along the way, until he turned back again.
They rode in harmony together, Abraham and Isaac, until they came to Mount Moriah.
But Abraham prepared everything for the sacrifice, calm and gentle, but as he turned away and drew the knife, then Isaac saw that Abraham's left hand was clenched in despair, that a trembling went through his whole body – but Abraham drew the knife.
Then they returned home again, and Sarah hurried to meet them, but Isaac had lost the faith.
Never in the world has a word been said about it, and Isaac never spoke to any human being of what he had seen, and Abraham did not suspect that anyone had seen it.

Thus and in many similar ways did that man of whom we speak think about this event.
