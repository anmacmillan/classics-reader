The works of the gods are full of providence.
The works of fortune are not apart from nature, or from the spinning-together and interweaving of the things governed by providence.
All things flow from there;
and joined to them is what is necessary and what is beneficial to the whole cosmos, of which you are a part.
And for every part of nature that is good which the nature of the whole brings and which is preservative of it.
And the changes of the compounds preserve the cosmos, just as do those of the elements.
Let these things be enough for you, and let them be your doctrines.
But throw away your thirst for books, so that you may not die grumbling, but truly gracious and thankful to the gods from the heart.
