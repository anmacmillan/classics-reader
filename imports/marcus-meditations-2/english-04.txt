Remember how long you have been putting these things off, and how many times, having received appointed days from the gods, you do not use them.
But it is necessary now at last to perceive of what cosmos you are a part, and of what governor of the cosmos you have subsisted as an emanation, and that a limit of time has been circumscribed for you, which if you do not use to clear the sky within you, it will be gone, and you will be gone, and it will not be possible again.
