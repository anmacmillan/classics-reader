Whatever this is that I am, it is bits of flesh and a little breath and the ruling faculty.
Despise the bits of flesh:
gore and little bones and a mesh, a little plait woven of nerves, small veins, arteries.
And look at the breath too, what sort of thing it is:
wind, and not even always the same, but every hour vomited out and gulped back again.
Third, then, is the ruling faculty.
Think of it this way:
you are an old man;
no longer let this be a slave, no longer let it be pulled like a puppet by unsociable impulse, no longer let it be discontented with what is fated or present, or look with suspicion on what is to come.
