Every hour take thought, sturdily, as a Roman and a man, to do what is in hand with exact and unfeigned dignity and affection and freedom and justice, and to provide yourself leisure from all other impressions.
And you will provide it, if you perform each action as the last of your life, freed from all randomness and passionate turning away from the choosing reason, and from hypocrisy and self-love and discontent with what has been allotted to you.
You see how few the things are which one who has mastered them can live a life flowing well and god-fearing;
for even the gods will demand nothing more from him who keeps these things.
