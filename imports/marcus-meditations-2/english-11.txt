As if it were possible right now to depart from life, so do and say and think each thing.
But to go away from among men, if there are gods, is nothing terrible;
for they would not involve you in evil;
and if either they do not exist or they do not care for human affairs, what is it to me to live in a universe empty of gods or empty of providence?
But in fact they do exist, and they do care for human affairs, and, so that man might not fall into the things that are truly evil, they have placed the whole matter in his own power;
and as for the rest, if anything were evil, they would have provided for this too, that it be in every man's power not to fall into it.
But what does not make a man worse — how could this make a man's life worse?
Neither through ignorance, nor knowing but being unable to guard against these things or set them right, could the nature of the whole have overlooked them; nor would it have erred so greatly, whether through want of power or want of skill, that good things and evil things should befall good men and bad men alike, indiscriminately mixed.
Yet death and life, good repute and ill repute, pain and pleasure, wealth and poverty — all these befall the good and the bad among men alike, being neither noble nor shameful.
Therefore they are neither good nor evil.
