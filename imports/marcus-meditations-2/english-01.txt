Say to yourself at dawn:
I shall meet with a meddler, an ingrate, a bully, a deceiver, an envier, an unsociable man;
all these things have befallen them through ignorance of goods and evils.
But I, having observed that the nature of the good is the beautiful, and that of the evil the shameful, and that the nature of the wrongdoer himself is akin to me — not of the same blood or seed, but a sharer in mind and a portion of the divine — can neither be harmed by any of them;
for no one will wrap me in what is shameful;
nor can I be angry with my kinsman or hate him.
For we have come into being for cooperation, like feet, like hands, like eyelids, like the rows of upper and lower teeth.
To work against one another, then, is against nature;
and to be vexed and to turn away is working against.
