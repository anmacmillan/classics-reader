That I am in fact the kind of man to have been given to the city by the god, you could see from this:
it does not seem merely human that I have neglected all my own affairs and put up with the neglect of my household for so many years now, while always doing your business, going to each of you privately like a father or an elder brother, persuading you to care about virtue.
And if I got something out of it and gave this advice for pay, there would be some reason in it;
but as it is you can see for yourselves that my accusers, shameless as they were in all their other accusations, could not bring themselves to be shameless enough on this point, by producing a witness that I ever charged or asked anyone for a fee.
For I produce, I think, a sufficient witness that I am telling the truth: my poverty.
It may perhaps seem strange that I go round giving this advice in private and busying myself, but in public I do not dare to go up before your assembly and advise the city.
The reason for this is what you have often heard me say in many places: that something divine and spiritual comes to me, a voice, which is just what Meletus wrote into his indictment, making fun of it.
For me this began in childhood: a sort of voice comes, and whenever it comes, it always turns me away from whatever I am about to do, but never urges me on.
This is what opposes my taking part in politics, and it seems to me that it is quite right to oppose it;
for be sure of this, men of Athens: if I had long ago tried to take part in politics, I would long ago have perished and would have done no good either to you or to myself.
And do not be angry with me for telling the truth;
for there is no man who will survive if he genuinely opposes you or any other people's assembly and tries to prevent many unjust and unlawful things from happening in the city; rather, anyone who really fights for what is just, if he is to survive even for a short time, must live as a private citizen and not in public life.
