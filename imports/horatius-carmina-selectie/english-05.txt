I have completed a monument more lasting than bronze
and loftier than the royal structure of the pyramids,
which no devouring rain, no unbridled North Wind
can tear down, nor the countless
succession of years and the flight of time.
I shall not wholly die, and a great part of me
will escape Libitina; on and on shall I
grow, ever fresh in the praise of later ages, as long as the Capitol
is climbed by the high priest with the silent Vestal virgin.
I shall be spoken of, where the violent Aufidus roars
and where Daunus, poor in water, ruled over rustic
peoples, as one who rose from humble birth to power,
the first to have brought Aeolian song to Italian
measures. Take up the pride
earned by merit, and with Delphic
laurel, Melpomene, graciously crown my hair.
