Now is the time for drinking, now with a free foot
to beat the earth; now with Salian feasts
to deck the couches of the gods
was the time long due, my comrades.
Before this it was sinful to bring out the Caecuban
from our grandfathers' cellars, while for the Capitol
a queen was plotting mad ruin
and death for the empire
with her polluted herd of men foul
with disease, reckless enough to hope for anything
and drunk with sweet
fortune. But her frenzy was lessened
when scarcely one ship escaped the flames,
and her mind, crazed with Mareotic wine,
was driven back to real fears
by Caesar, as she flew from Italy
and he pressed hard with oars, as a hawk
pursues soft doves, or a swift hunter the hare
on the plains of snowy
Thessaly, to put in chains
that fatal monster. But she, seeking to die
more nobly, did not, as women do,
shrink in terror from the sword, nor with her swift fleet
make for hidden shores.
She dared to look upon her fallen palace
with a calm face, and bravely to handle
the rough serpents, so that she might drink
the black venom into her body,
fiercer once she had resolved on death;
she begrudged, no doubt, the cruel Liburnian galleys
the chance to lead her, no longer a queen, in a proud
triumph: no humble woman.
