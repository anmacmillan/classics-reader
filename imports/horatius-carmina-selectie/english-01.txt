Do you see how Soracte stands there, white with deep
snow, how the labouring woods can no longer bear
their burden, and how with the sharp frost
the rivers have come to a standstill?
Thaw out the cold, piling logs upon the hearth
generously, and more lavishly still
draw off the four-year-old from the Sabine
two-handled jar, O Thaliarchus, unmixed.
Leave the rest to the gods: as soon as they
have laid the winds that battle it out on the seething
sea, neither the cypresses
nor the old ash trees are tossed about.
What tomorrow will bring, avoid asking, and
whatever day Chance grants you, set it down
as profit; and do not spurn sweet loves,
while you are a boy, nor dances either,
so long as grey hair keeps away from your green youth,
peevish grey. Now let the Campus and the squares
and soft whispers at nightfall
be sought again at the appointed hour;
now too, betraying a girl who hides, from an inmost
corner comes her welcome laugh,
and a token snatched from her arm
or from a finger that barely holds on.
