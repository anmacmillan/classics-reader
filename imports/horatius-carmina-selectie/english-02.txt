Do not ask (to know is forbidden) what end for me, what end for you
the gods have given, Leuconoe, and do not try Babylonian
numbers. How much better to endure whatever will be,
whether Jupiter has granted more winters or this as the last,
which now wears out the sea against the porous cliffs that face it,
the Tyrrhenian: be wise, strain your wine, and to a brief span
cut back long hope. While we talk, envious
time will have fled: pluck the day, trusting as little as possible in tomorrow.
