Towards daybreak she came and opened the door.
When I asked why the doors had been creaking in the night, she said the lamp by the baby had gone out and she had lit it again at the neighbours'.
I said nothing and believed it was so.
It did strike me, gentlemen, that her face was made up, though her brother had died less than thirty days before.
Even so, I said nothing about it, and went out and left without a word.
