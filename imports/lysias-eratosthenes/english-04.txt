Some time later, gentlemen, I came back from the country unexpectedly, and after dinner the baby was crying and fretting; the slave girl was pinching it on purpose to make it do so.
The man was inside the house;
I found all this out later.
I told my wife to go down and give the baby the breast, so it would stop crying.
At first she refused, as if she were glad to see me home after so long.
When I got angry and told her to go, she said, “Oh yes, so that you can have a go at the little maid here!
You grabbed at her before, when you were drunk.”
I laughed, and she got up, went out, closed the door as if in fun, and drew the key after her.
I thought nothing of it and suspected nothing, and I slept soundly, having come in from the country.
