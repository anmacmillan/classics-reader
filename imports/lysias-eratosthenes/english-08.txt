After that four or five days went by, as I shall prove to you by strong evidence.
But first I want to tell you what happened on the last day.
Sostratos was a close friend of mine.
I met him after sunset as he was coming in from the country.
Knowing that, arriving at that hour, he would find none of his friends at home, I invited him to dine with me.
We went to my house, climbed up to the upper room and had dinner.
When he had had enough, he left and went home, and I went to sleep.
