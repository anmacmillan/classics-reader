I believe then, gentlemen, that this punishment was not a private matter for my own sake, but for the sake of the whole city.
For men who do such things, seeing what rewards await such crimes, will be less inclined to wrong others, if they see that you share the same view.
Otherwise it would be far better to wipe out the laws in force and make others that will punish men who guard their own wives, and give full licence to those who want to wrong them.
That would be far more just than for citizens to be trapped by the laws, which tell a man who catches an adulterer to do with him whatever he likes, while the trials turn out more dangerous for the wronged than for those who, against the laws, dishonour other men's wives.
For now I stand in danger of my life, my property and everything else, because I obeyed the laws of the city.
