Now consider this, gentlemen:
they accuse me of ordering the slave girl to fetch the young man on that day.
For my part, gentlemen, I would have thought myself in the right to catch the man who corrupted my wife by any means at all.
If I had had him fetched when only words had been spoken and nothing had actually happened, I would have been in the wrong;
but when everything had already been done and he had often come into my house, I would have thought myself fully justified in catching him by any means at all.
But consider that this too is a lie;
you will see it easily from what follows.
As I told you before, gentlemen, Sostratos, who is my friend and on close terms with me, met me as he came in from the country around sunset and dined with me, and when he had had enough, he went away.
Now think about this first, gentlemen:
if I had been plotting against Eratosthenes that night, would it have been better for me to dine somewhere else myself, or to bring a guest home to dine with me?
For then he would have been less likely to dare to enter the house.
And then, do you think I would have let my dinner guest go and been left alone and without help, instead of asking him to stay so that he could help me punish the adulterer?
Then again, gentlemen, don't you think I would have sent word to my friends during the day and told them to gather at the house of some friend living as close as possible, rather than run round in the night the moment I found out, not knowing whom I would find at home and whom away?
As it was, I went to Harmodios and so-and-so, who were out of town (I didn't know), and found others not at home, and I took along those I could get.
Yet if I had known beforehand, don't you think I would have got servants ready and sent word to my friends, so that I could go in as safely as possible myself (for how was I to know whether he had a knife too?) and exact the penalty with as many witnesses as possible?
But in fact I knew nothing of what was going to happen that night, and I took along whoever I could.
Witnesses to this, please step up.
