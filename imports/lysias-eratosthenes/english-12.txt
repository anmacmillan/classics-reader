In my case, then, gentlemen, the laws have not only acquitted me of wrongdoing: they have actually ordered me to take this satisfaction.
It is up to you whether these laws are to be strong or worth nothing.
For I believe every city makes its laws so that when we are at a loss, we can go to them and see what we ought to do.
And in cases like this, these laws urge the injured party to take just this kind of satisfaction.
I ask you to take the same view as they do.
Otherwise you will give adulterers such licence that you will encourage even thieves to claim they are adulterers, knowing full well that if they give this reason for themselves and say it was for this that they entered other men's houses, nobody will lay a finger on them.
Everyone will know that the laws on adultery can be ignored, and that it is your vote they need to fear,
