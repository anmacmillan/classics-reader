Now first of all, gentlemen (I have to explain this too), my house is a small one on two floors, the upper equal in size to the lower, with the women's rooms upstairs and the men's rooms downstairs.
When our baby was born, its mother nursed it herself.
So that she would not risk going down the stairs whenever it needed washing, I took to living upstairs and the women downstairs.
It had become such a habit that my wife often went down to sleep beside the baby, to give it the breast and stop it crying.
Things went on like this for a long time, and I never suspected a thing; I was such a fool that I thought my wife the most virtuous woman in the city.
