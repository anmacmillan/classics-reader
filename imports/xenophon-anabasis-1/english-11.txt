and Xenias the Arcadian general and Pasion the Megarian boarded a ship, put their most valuable possessions on it and sailed away, offended in their pride, as most people thought, because Cyrus was letting Clearchus keep their soldiers, who had gone over to Clearchus in the belief that they were going back to Greece and not against the King.
When they had disappeared, a rumour went round that Cyrus was pursuing them with triremes;
and some prayed that they would be caught, since they were cowards, while others pitied them if they should be captured.
But Cyrus called the generals together and said:
‘Xenias and Pasion have deserted us.
But let them know well that they have not run away from me;
for I know where they are going;
nor have they escaped;
for I have triremes enough to catch their ship;
but by the gods, I will not pursue them, and no one shall say that I make use of a man as long as he is with me, but when he wants to leave, I seize him, treat him badly and rob him of his property.
No, let them go, knowing that they are behaving worse towards us than we towards them.
And yet I do have their children and wives under guard in Tralles;
but they shall not lose even these: they shall get them back, because of their earlier good service to me.’
That is what he said;
and the Greeks, even any who had been rather disheartened about the march up country, when they heard of Cyrus’ generosity, marched on with him more gladly and more eagerly.
After this Cyrus marched four stages, twenty parasangs, to the river Chalus, which was a plethron wide and full of large, tame fish, which the Syrians regarded as gods and would not allow anyone to harm, nor the doves either.
The villages in which they camped belonged to Parysatis, given to her for her girdle-money.
From there he marched five stages, thirty parasangs, to the springs of the river Dardas, which was a plethron wide.
There was the palace of Belesys, who had been governor of Syria, and a very large and beautiful park, containing everything that the seasons produce.
Cyrus cut it down and burned the palace.
